office life; (3) punchline last; (4) one line, 1–200 chars; plus freshness
and tightness. Lessons applied: maximally compressed, mystery left
UNANSWERED, one concrete image, concrete time/place/character, shorter wins.

## Winner

#13, polished: **"Our mindfulness coach lasted exactly one meeting with Finance."**
(62 characters)

Polish round: "quit after his first meeting" → "lasted exactly one meeting".
"Lasted" does the quitting work in one word, "exactly" sharpens the
single-meeting absurdity, and the line drops from 66 to 62 characters while
leaving WHY Finance broke the coach unanswered. Punchline ("Finance") is
last; one concrete character (the coach) and one concrete department.
